Business Pulse

See your business clearly. One executive dashboard for sales, revenue and receivables, built natively into Odoo.

Stop jumping between Sales, Invoicing and reports to understand how the company is doing. Business Pulse brings the numbers that matter into one fast, clear workspace. Every figure links to the exact records behind it, and every number follows the access rights of the person looking at it.

Odoo 18.0 Tested on Community Requires Sales + Invoicing No external services

Everything an owner or manager checks every morning

Configurable KPI cards

Confirmed sales, orders, average order value, conversion rate, open quotations, active and new customers, invoiced revenue, receivables and overdue receivables. Each card has a trend sparkline, a comparison and a plain-language definition.

Honest period comparisons

Compare with the previous period, the same period last year or any custom range. Month-to-date is compared with the same days of last month, so partial periods are never compared with full ones. Rates change in percentage points (pp), not misleading percentages.

Drill down to the records

Click any card, chart point, bar, ranking row, aging bucket or alert to open exactly the orders or invoices behind that number, with the same filters. Use the breadcrumb to come back with your filters intact.

Sales trend, breakdown and top performers

An interactive trend chart (sales, orders or invoiced revenue) with automatic or manual day/week/month/quarter grouping and a table view. Break sales down by category, product, salesperson, team, customer or company. Rank top products, customers and salespeople.

Receivables aging

Outstanding and overdue balances with Not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ day buckets, and the customers with the largest overdue balances. Based on real residual amounts, not on assumptions.

Needs-attention alerts

Deterministic, configurable rules: quotations about to expire, expired or stalled quotations, sales decline beyond a threshold, overdue and long-overdue invoices. Each alert explains the issue and links to the next step.

Secure by design

  • All figures are computed on the server with the user's own access rights and record rules. A salesperson limited to their own documents only sees their own numbers.
  • Financial sections are only shown to users with an Invoicing or Accounting role.
  • Multi-company aware: the company switcher and filters are respected, and amounts are converted to the active company's currency.
  • Excel, CSV and PDF exports apply the same filters and permissions as the screen.
  • Salespeople limited to their own documents only see their own personal targets.
  • No data leaves your Odoo server and no external services are used.

Filters that apply everywhere

Thirteen date presets plus custom ranges, and filters for company, sales team, salesperson, customer, product and product category. Active filters appear as removable chips. When a filter does not apply to a section (for example a product filter on receivables), the section says so instead of hiding it.

Sales Analytics: from quotation to target

A dedicated page for sales managers: how quotations convert, where the pipeline is ageing, and how every salesperson is doing against their target.

Quotation funnel

Quotations created in the period and what happened to them: confirmed, still open or cancelled, with values, conversion versus the comparison period and average days to confirm. Uses a clear cohort rule, so periods are never mixed.

Open pipeline health

Open quotations by age (0–7, 8–30, 31–90, 90+ days), quotations expiring soon, expired quotations still open, and the highest-value deals waiting for a decision.

Salesperson performance

A sortable table with confirmed sales, change, orders, average order value, conversion, open pipeline and progress against each person's target.

Targets that mean something

  • Set monthly, quarterly, yearly or custom targets for confirmed sales, orders or invoiced revenue.
  • Targets can apply to the whole company, a sales team or one salesperson.
  • Progress is measured exactly like the matching KPI, in the company currency.
  • Each target shows its completion, the amount still to go, and a marker for the expected pro-rata progress. Status is On track, Behind, Achieved, Missed or Not started.

Saved views and reports

  • Saved views: save the current page, period, comparison and filters under a name, and reopen them in one click. Each user's views are private.
  • Excel export with real number, currency and percentage formats, report details, frozen headers and filters.
  • CSV export for any other tool.
  • Print / Save as PDF: a clean, paginated report of the current page.

Personal, without compromising data

Each user chooses and orders up to 12 KPI cards and sets a personal default period and comparison. Administrators set company defaults, alert thresholds and export limits in Settings. Personalization changes what is displayed, never which records a user can see.

Built for daily use

  • Each section loads independently, with loading, empty, error and restricted states. One slow section never blocks the others.
  • Keyboard accessible, with visible focus, screen-reader labels and a table view for the chart. Status is never shown by color alone.
  • Responsive from wide desktop screens to phones.
  • Native Odoo look and feel, built with Odoo's own OWL framework and Chart.js library.

Metric definitions

Confirmed SalesUntaxed value of confirmed sales order lines whose order date is in the period, in the active company currency. Cancelled orders are excluded.
Confirmed OrdersNumber of confirmed sales orders whose order date is in the period.
Average Order ValueConfirmed Sales divided by Confirmed Orders.
Conversion RateOf all quotations created in the period, the share that is now a confirmed order (cohort-based).
Invoiced RevenueUntaxed amount of posted customer invoices minus posted credit notes, by invoice date. This is billed revenue, not cash collected.
Outstanding / Overdue ReceivablesResidual amount of posted customer invoices and credit notes as of today. Overdue means the due date is before today.

Installation and requirements

  • Odoo 18.0. Tested on Community edition, on-premise. It only depends on standard apps, so it is intended for Odoo.sh and Enterprise as well. Odoo Online (SaaS) does not allow custom modules.
  • Depends on the Sales and Invoicing apps. No extra Python libraries are required.
  • After installation, open the Business Pulse app. Sales Administrators get access automatically. Give other users the Business Pulse / User access right.
  • Configure defaults and alert thresholds in Business Pulse → Configuration → Settings.

Known limitations

  • Amounts in other currencies are converted at the latest exchange rate, the same way Odoo's Sales Analysis report does.
  • Receivables are a snapshot as of today; the date range does not apply to them, and unallocated payments are not deducted.
  • Profitability, inventory, budgets, shared dashboards and scheduled reports are not part of this version.
  • "Average days to confirm" uses the order date, which Odoo sets when a quotation is confirmed.
